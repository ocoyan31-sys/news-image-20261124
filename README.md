# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A single image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026,
entered in the make.fun News Image 24 Nov competition (news-image-20261124).

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is a front page with six stories from that day:

1. **NVIDIA** — Jensen Huang returns as CEO after Trump intervenes; shares jump ~30%.
2. **COP31** — climate talks in Antalya collapse with no fossil-fuel deal.
3. **Ukraine war** — Ukrainian naval drones sink a Russian warship off Libya.
4. **World Court** — the ICJ finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya.
5. **US trial** — Huawei found guilty of trade-secret theft and bank fraud.
6. **Gaza** — Israeli strikes kill Hamas internal security chief in Gaza City.

Also noted: a Turkish military helicopter crash kills five; the US loses its
measles elimination status after 25+ years.

Sources: Daily Summary Of (24 Nov 2026 edition), The Daily Edition No. 279,
The Global Eye, Principia, CWEB Daily Shocker (24 Nov 2026).

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run it twice and the outputs are byte-identical.
